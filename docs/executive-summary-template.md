# 1-Page Executive Summary Template

> **Note for Teams:** To minimize documentation burden and maximize engineering time, this **1-page executive summary** alongside your pitch deck replaces the need for a lengthy 40-page project report! Keep it sharp, factual, and strictly under one to two pages.

---

## [Project Name]: [One-Sentence Value Proposition]

- **Team Name:** `[Your Team Name]`
- **Track / Problem Statement:** `[PS1-PS6 or Open Innovation - Title]`
- **Repository URL:** `https://github.com/[org-or-user]/[repo-name]`
- **Live Demo / Video Link:** `[YouTube / Deployed URL]`
- **Team Members & Roles:**
  1. `Member 1 (Lead & Software)`
  2. `Member 2 (Hardware / Embedded)`
  3. `Member 3 (ML / Data Science)`
  4. `Member 4 (UI/UX / Testing)`
  5. `Member 5 (Domain Research)`

---

### 1. Problem & Agricultural Reality (150 words)
- What specific bottleneck or loss do farmers face?
- What are the economic or resource costs of leaving this unsolved?

### 2. Proposed Technical Solution (200 words)
- What did your team build?
- Highlight the integration of hardware, software, AI, or data analytics.
- What makes your solution cost-effective and farmer-friendly?

### 3. Key Accomplishments & Working Prototype (150 words)
- What is fully functional today? (e.g. "Working ESP32 sensor node streaming to FastAPI backend with 92% accurate CNN disease classifier on Raspberry Pi")
- Results from validation or testing: What benchmarks or metrics did you hit?

### 4. Hardware & Unit Economics (100 words)
- Prototype Unit Cost: (e.g., ₹1,500 / $18 USD)
- Estimated Farmer ROI / Payback Period: (e.g., 4 months via 30% water savings and reduced pumping electricity)

### 5. Roadmap & Next Steps (100 words)
- Pilot deployment plan with actual farmers or FPOs.
- Planned technical improvements (e.g. PCB manufacturing, multilingual voice assistant).
