# Presentation & Pitch Deck Guidelines

Your presentation is a critical element of how judges evaluate your solution. Follow these guidelines to build an impactful, concise pitch deck.

- **In-Repository Path:** Save your exported PDF directly as:
  [`docs/presentation.pdf`](file:///Users/laku/projects/agripreneurs-buildathon-template/docs/presentation.pdf)
- **Cloud Link Alternative:** If hosted externally (Google Slides, Canva, Notion), include the public view link directly in [`README.md`](file:///Users/laku/projects/agripreneurs-buildathon-template/README.md) and on the final text submission form. ---

## Live Pitch & Q&A Rules
- **Pitch Duration:** 5-7 minutes presentation + 3-5 minutes judge Q&A (verify exact schedule with organizers).
- **Speakers Rule:** **Maximum 3 speakers** from the team during the live pitch and Q&A session. Other team members may attend to observe or assist with technical demonstrations.
- **Team Size:** Recommended 2–5 members per team.

---

## Recommended Slide Structure (8–10 Slides Max)

### Slide 1: Title & Hook
- Project Name, Catchy One-liner, Track (PS1-PS6 or Open Innovation), Team Name & Member Names.

### Slide 2: The Problem & On-Ground Reality
- What is the specific agricultural bottleneck? Who suffers? Provide real-world numbers (e.g. crop yield lost, water wasted, labor cost).

### Slide 3: Your Solution & Value Proposition
- What did you create? High-level overview of the solution and its core mechanism.

### Slide 4: Working Prototype / Product Demo (Screenshots & Video Link)
- Highlight working proof: UI screenshots, photos of hardware breadboard/assembly, telemetry graph, or embedded 10-second GIF.
- Link to your 2-3 minute unlisted YouTube demo video.

### Slide 5: System Architecture & Technical Depth
- How the pieces connect: Sensors $\rightarrow$ Edge/MCU $\rightarrow$ Cloud/Server $\rightarrow$ AI model $\rightarrow$ Farmer interface.
- Show off your engineering chops!

### Slide 6: Field Feasibility & Farmer Economics
- Bill of Materials (BOM) cost (e.g. ₹1,500).
- Expected savings or yield increase for the farmer and payback period (e.g., recovers cost in 1 harvest cycle).

### Slide 7: Challenges, Learnings & Novelty
- Technical hurdles overcome during the buildathon (e.g. sensor calibration, power constraints, model optimization).
- Why your approach outperforms standard methods.

### Slide 8: Roadmap & Future Vision
- Next 3 milestones (e.g. pilot with 10 local farmers, vernacular voice bot integration, PCB fabrication).

### Slide 9: Team & Roles
- Photos, names, degree/college, and specific roles played in this buildathon.
- Maximum 3 designated speakers for Q&A identified.
